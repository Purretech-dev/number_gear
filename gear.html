Number Gear 2

Select a colored plane, then move it with the arrows.

Gear practice

Arrange the 11 to 220 number gear

Start at the top guide. The inner plane reads 11-20. Each plane outside it adds the same number again, so the top line becomes 11, 22, 33, 44, 55, 66, 77, 88, 99, 110, 121. Mix the gear, choose one plane counter, then turn that plane until its numbers line up with the guide. Reset returns every plane to the correct starting position.

Level 5

Prime Number Gear

Select a prime plane, then move it with the arrows.

Gear practice

Arrange the prime number gear

Prime numbers are greater than 1 and have exactly two factors: 1 and the number itself. This gear shows the first 100 prime numbers in 10 colored planes. Mix the planes, choose a plane counter, then turn each plane until the first prime number on that plane reaches the top guide.
